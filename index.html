💛 About TMFX LotLock

This calculator takes care of about 90% of what a newbie to forex actually needs to get started with confidence.

You get your Support/Resistance — Daily, Weekly, and Monthly — at the snap of your fingers, each with its own dotted mid-level values between every zone for finer read on price. No more spending endless hours "studying" a new currency pair before you feel ready to touch it. You have access to almost 40 currency pairs, so you're not boxed into just the majors. You can work out your lot size instantly, based on exactly how much you want to trade with, in dollars or in cents — with an instant Approval check (OK or Low Funds) so you know immediately if your numbers actually work. All the tedious groundwork — the math, the manual pivot calculations, the spread buffer — is already taken care of for you, in both Auto (live price feed) and Manual modes, independently on every step.

We've also built in the Magic Room 1 — a set of pre-mapped price levels for each pair, broken into nine timeframe bands (1min through Monthly), plus two special bands that automatically save your actual Daily/Weekly/Monthly R/S levels in the background the moment you calculate them — so they're there for you even if you're working in Manual mode. Each saved level shows exactly when it was generated and when it expires, so you always know if you're looking at something fresh. To be upfront about the core grid itself: it's our own proprietary level system, built from each pair's historical price range rather than pulled from a live daily/weekly/monthly candle. We're not going to claim it's "mathematically proven stronger" than standard R/S — that's not a claim we can back up with independent testing, and we'd rather you trust us because we're straight with you than oversell it. What we can say honestly is that it gives you a consistent, ready-made grid of reference levels for every pair, and traders who've used it find real value checking price against it. Access to the Magic Room 1 is protected by your account password, re-entered each session, to keep it just between you and your account.

Your Magic Room 1 picks don't have to stay stuck in a phone screen either. Every instrument you've saved values for in the TMFX Box can now be turned into a downloadable file for desktop MetaTrader (MT5 and MT4) that draws exactly your saved lines — grouped and colored by timeframe — straight onto your chart, so you're never manually redrawing them by hand. And if you're on your phone without MetaTrader installed, the same saved values are available as a live price chart right inside the app, timeframe by timeframe, so you can see how price is actually sitting against your levels wherever you are.

Everything you calculate — R/S or Lot results — can be saved to your History, which now actually persists between visits instead of resetting every time you reopen the app, with full control to delete individual entries or clear it all.

This is a love project. We built it because every other tool made us do too much manual work for too little insight, and we wanted something that actually respects a trader's time. We believe it's one of the most complete free tools of its kind — we won't tell you nothing else compares, because we haven't tested everything else out there, but we built this to be genuinely indispensable to your daily routine.

Free for your first 200 active traders. Open a live account through our affiliate link, stay an active trader, and you keep free access for as long as you keep trading. This is a real relationship, not a bait-and-switch: if we find clear signs of inactivity or misuse, access can be withdrawn — but that's about protecting a genuine offer for genuine traders, not a hidden catch.
